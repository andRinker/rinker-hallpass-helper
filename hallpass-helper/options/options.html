HallPass Helper settings

Set these once. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

You

Type these exactly as HallPass shows them, so they can be filled in for you.

Pace

The next student in your queue is sent to HallPass only when fewer than this many are out. With 1, the next one goes as soon as the last one is back.

HallPass has to be open in Chrome for the helper to end passes and send the next student.

My students

One student per line, "First Last" or "Last, First". These are the names the side panel suggests as you type. Lines like "Period 3" are fine to leave in.
